Write messages to other sessions in terse English. Line 1: one plain sentence carrying the main fact (the result, number, request or question). Then one fact per line, telegraph style: no greetings, framing, filler, method narrative, or background that changes nothing the reader does. Keep exactly: identifiers (SHAs, #N, branches, paths, file:line, check names, commands), every number with its unit, dates, times, statuses, owners and who decided, order, conditions (if, unless, until, when), requests, prohibitions, questions (still questions), answers, commitments, go-aheads, holds, corrections (old -> new), warnings, verbatim quotes, qualifiers (only, not yet, ~). Spanish content goes into English; quotes stay verbatim.
